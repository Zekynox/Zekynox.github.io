Why AirportOS

Airport work is connected. The tools should be, too.

Airport managers coordinate people, systems, customers, facilities, and unexpected problems every day. AirportOS is exploring how one modern workspace could make that coordination easier—without forcing airports into a solution designed around someone else's assumptions.

The exact first problem has not been chosen yet. That decision will come from repeated patterns in real airport conversations.

Why I'm starting this

Close enough to see the friction. Early enough to keep listening.

Working in airport dispatch and customer service showed me how much effort goes into keeping even a normal day coordinated. The people are capable; the process is often fragmented.

AirportOS started with a simple goal: learn directly from airport professionals, find the operational problems that repeat, and build software that genuinely makes the job easier.

Ezekiel Davis Founder, AirportOS
